Synergy Enterprises FCMG (HR Process Optimization)

Synergy Enterprises is a diversified multinational manufacturing (FCMG) with operations spanning five continents and a workforce exceeding 75,000 employees. The Company strategically aims to build a consistent and strong global brand image, enhance customer relationships across all continents, and increase customer loyalty through high-quality products, excellent service, and understanding diverse market needs. Synergy's success hinges on its ability to attract, develop, and retain top talent globally. Despite its global reach, Synergy's Human Resources (HR) function currently operates with a fragmented technology landscape. Each major region or individual businesses had seperate HR systems, ranging from legacy on-premise solutions to disparate cloud- based tools for specific functions (e.g., one system for recruitment, another for payroll, and spreadsheets for performance reviews). Maintaining many HR systems requires significant IT resources and budget. We came up with a brilliant Business case that introduced access to a single accurate picture of all employees for better decision making, Automate HR related processes to reduce error rate and improve process efficiency, Standardized HR Practices across the various continent leading to similar experience for the Staff. We used a hybrid methology for implementation that proved efficient for a quick roll out

Barrel & Brew Operations Transformation (uSING BRD, Gap Analysis, Process Mapping)

Barrel & Brew is a popular, family-owned craft winery. As business analysts, we solved Barrel & Brew's growth problems in three connected steps. Gap analysis: Comparing the current state with leadership's vision exposed clear gaps: missed production targets, inconsistent batches, handwritten records, intuition-based forecasting, ageing equipment and poor sales–production communication. A fishbone diagram grouped these root causes by People, Process, Data, Technology and Equipment. Process mapping: Mapping the brewing flow from recipe planning to bottling revealed manual hand-offs and no link between sales orders and production capacity. A future-state swim-lane diagram assigned clear roles, automated inventory updates and added quality and availability checks. BRD: Our Business Requirements Document specified an integrated system for digital recipe tracking, real-time inventory, data-driven forecasting and shared dashboards, plus an equipment upgrade plan, moving Barrel & Brew from reactive firefighting to scalable, sustainable growth.
